In recent decades, technologies such as Wi-Fi®, IoT, PoE, OT and AI have arisen and evolved. Remote work and increased flexibility have reshaped perceptions of workspaces, demanding more adaptability from networks. The demand for network access has expanded beyond traditional office and data center environments to include warehouses, parking lots, sports venues and virtually any location where people and devices require connectivity.
